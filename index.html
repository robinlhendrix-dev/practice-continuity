01 | Recognition

Continuity risks often hide inside ordinary technology

A practice can look stable while important access, knowledge, and client steps depend on one person or an aging setup.

Signal 01

Access lives in too many places

Domains, hosting, scheduling, and email may be controlled through accounts nobody has documented.

Signal 02

The website no longer reflects the practice

Years of experience and current services are represented by an older version of the business.

Signal 03

A vendor holds the working knowledge

Small changes depend on the availability of someone who may not be part of the next chapter.

Signal 04

The client path has accumulated friction

Contact, scheduling, intake, and telehealth may work, but not as one understandable experience.

Signal 05

Support is reactive

The practice needs a person to call before a small technical issue becomes an interruption.
